AutoCut 私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 28 日

簡單講

AutoCut 唔收集你嘅個人資料。你嘅片、逐字稿、字幕、文案同設定全部只存喺你部機。冇帳戶、冇廣告、冇追蹤、冇分析工具。

喺你部機處理嘅資料

  • 你揀嘅影片同相片：只喺本機剪接同分析（例如轉錄、搵人臉位置）。
  • 逐字稿、字幕、AI 寫嘅標題同文案：存喺 App 嘅資料夾。
  • 你回報嘅 AI 內容：存喺本機，唔會自動傳出去。你可以自己決定經支援頁話我哋知。

會連網嘅情況（全部由你啟動）

  • 下載 AI 模型：你撳「下載」之後，App 會由 Hugging Face 或 GitHub 下載模型檔。呢啲網站會見到你嘅 IP 地址（同普通下載一樣），但 App 唔會傳送你嘅片或任何個人資料。
  • 打開平台網頁：你批准發佈之後，App 會用你嘅瀏覽器開 Instagram、YouTube 等網站。上載由你自己做，受該平台嘅私隱政策約束。
  • 可選功能：如果你自己喺設定輸入 Pexels API key，搜尋關鍵字會傳去 Pexels 去搵免費素材。
  • YouTube 上載（可選，Mac）：如果你自己連接 YouTube 帳戶，你批准嘅片同標題會經 YouTube 官方 API 上載去你嘅頻道。登入資料只存喺你部 Mac。
  • iPhone 語音辨識：有離線辨識嘅語言全部喺 iPhone 處理；冇離線辨識嘅語言（設定會寫「要連網」），聲音會交俾 Apple 語音服務辨識，受 Apple 私隱政策約束，唔會經我哋。

兒童

AutoCut 唔係為 13 歲以下兒童設計，亦唔會有意收集兒童資料。

刪除資料

刪除 App 就會刪除佢嘅所有資料。剪好嘅片喺「影片 › AutoCut」，你可以自己刪除。

聯絡

有私隱問題：喺呢度話我哋知。政策有改會更新呢頁同生效日期。

AutoCut Privacy Policy

Effective 28 November 2026

In short

AutoCut does not collect personal data. Your videos, transcripts, subtitles, captions and settings stay on your device. There are no accounts, ads, tracking or analytics.

Data processed on your device

  • Videos and photos you choose are edited and analysed (for example transcription and face position) only on your device.
  • Transcripts, subtitles and AI-written titles and captions are stored in the app's folder.
  • Reports you make about AI content are stored locally and are not sent anywhere automatically.

When the app uses the network (always started by you)

  • Downloading AI models: after you tap Download, model files are fetched from Hugging Face or GitHub. These sites see your IP address as with any download; the app sends no videos or personal data.
  • Opening a platform: after you approve a post, the app opens Instagram, YouTube or another site in your browser. You upload yourself, under that platform's privacy policy.
  • Optional: if you enter your own Pexels API key, search keywords are sent to Pexels to find free stock footage.
  • YouTube upload (optional, Mac): if you connect your own YouTube account, clips and titles you approve are uploaded to your channel through YouTube's official API. Sign-in tokens stay on your Mac.
  • iPhone speech recognition: languages with on-device support are processed entirely on your iPhone. For other languages (shown as "needs network" in Settings) audio is sent to Apple's speech service under Apple's privacy policy, never to us.

Children

AutoCut is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting data

Deleting the app deletes all its data. Finished videos are in Movies › AutoCut, where you can delete them yourself.
