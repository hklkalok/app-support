AutoCut 支援

AutoCut 喺你部 Mac / iPhone 入面將長片剪成短片：自動轉錄（廣東話、普通話、英文）、加繁體或簡體字幕、剪走停頓、寫標題同文案。全部喺你部機運行，唔使帳戶。

聯絡我哋

有問題、建議或者想回報 AI 寫出嚟嘅不當內容：喺呢度開一個回報（要 GitHub 帳戶，免費）。我哋會喺 3 個工作天內回覆。請唔好貼私人資料或者影片內容。

常見問題

第一次用要下載咩？
轉錄同本機 AI 要下載模型（約 0.5–3 GB，視乎你揀邊個）。App 會先話你知大小，你撳「下載」先會開始。之後可以喺「設定 → AI 模型」刪除。

我嘅片會唔會上載？
唔會。剪片、轉錄、寫文案全部喺你部機做。詳情見私隱政策。

剪好嘅片放喺邊？
Mac：「影片 › AutoCut」。iPhone：App 入面嘅片庫，可以用分享按鈕存去相簿。

點樣發佈去 IG / YouTube？
每條 post 都要你自己批准。App 會抄好文案、開平台網頁、揀好條片，由你自己上載同撳發佈。App 唔會自動發佈。

可以用 YouTube 連結嗎？
App Store 版唔支援由網站下載片，只可以用你自己有權使用嘅影片檔。

AI 寫錯嘢點算？
AI 文案會先過濾嚴重冒犯字眼，但都可能出錯或者唔準。發佈前請自己睇過。用 App 入面「回報內容」或者上面嘅連結話我哋知。

AutoCut Support

AutoCut turns long videos into short clips on your own Mac or iPhone: automatic transcription (Cantonese, Mandarin, English), Traditional or Simplified Chinese subtitles, pause removal, and AI-written titles and captions. Everything runs on your device. No account needed.

Contact

For questions, feedback or to report objectionable AI-generated content, open a report here (free GitHub account required). We reply within 3 business days. Please don't include personal information or video content.

FAQ

What needs downloading? Speech and on-device AI models (about 0.5–3 GB depending on your choice). The app shows the size and only downloads after you tap Download. You can remove models in Settings › AI models.

Are my videos uploaded? No. Cutting, transcription and writing all happen on your device. See the privacy policy.

Does it post for me? No. You approve every post, and you upload it yourself.
